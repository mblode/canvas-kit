Lead with **Why** (the user's motivation), not **What** (the product).

## The three layers

| Layer    | Question it answers              | Example                                                |
| -------- | -------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------ |
| **Why**  | Why does this matter to me?      | _Stale dashboards kill trust._                         |
| **How**  | How does the product address it? | _StrataSync keeps every client in sync automatically._ |
| **What** | What is the product?             | _A real-time data layer for React apps._               |

## When to skip the Why

Onboarding: users already know why. Lead with How.

Pricing: the reader is shopping. Lead with the offer.

Reference docs: engineers want What and How.

## Common mistakes

The Why must be the _user's_ Why. "Our team is obsessed with developer experience" is founder voice. "You shouldn't have to babysit your pipeline" is user voice.

"We believe in a more connected world" is a mission statement. _Stale dashboards kill trust_ lands because the reader has lived it.
